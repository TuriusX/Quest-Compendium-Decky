# Changelog

## 0.19.4
- **Suggested sites for your game.** The Guides tab lists Steam guides, Fandom, GameFAQs, YouTube walkthroughs, an
  interactive map and Reddit for the game you're playing, under Quest Compendium's guide.
- **Resume guide remembers each game.** It reopens the last page you had open for the game you're playing.

## 0.19.3
- **See what to look for.** Each marked spot in the checklist and the full answer has a close-up from your screenshot.
- **Clearer marker names.** Markers say what the thing is instead of "Loot this" or "Proceed here".

## 0.19.2
- **Try Premium free for 7 days.** The Premium card now offers the free trial of monthly Premium (one per account,
  cancel any time before it ends and you won't be charged), and from November 26 to January 4 the holiday deal on
  yearly: $29.99 for the first year.
- **A reminder before your trial ends.** The day before, a note at the top of the panel says so.

## 0.19.1
- **Typing with the touch keyboard works better.** The question box no longer misses key presses while you type.
- **Install from DeckyHub.** Quest Compendium is on DeckyHub, which installs it and keeps it up to date.
- The logo now loads from questcompendium.com, and the plugin's security notes are in the README: no root, HTTPS
  always checked, and what it reads on your Deck.

## 0.19.0
- **A cleaner panel.** One card at the top with your game, where you are and your questions left, then three tabs:
  Ask, Quest log and Guides.
- **You can see it working.** After you ask, the panel shows what's happening (the screenshot taken, the answer being
  written by Pro or Fast) with the seconds so far and how long it usually takes. You can go back to the game; the
  answer waits.
- **The answer first.** The newest answer sits under Help me here, then its picture or video, then 👍 👎, the full
  answer with its sources, and Report on one row.
- **Quest log tab.** Your quest steps have their own tab instead of sitting under every answer.
- **Pages open in Quest Compendium.** Wiki pages and an answer's sources open in a full-screen reader, scrolled with
  the D-pad, even when Steam's browser won't draw. Steam's browser is still one press away.
- **Pictures full screen.** Press an answer's picture to see it full screen.
- The same purple as the Windows app.

## 0.18.0
- **Videos play in Quest Compendium.** Watch opens the video full screen on Quest Compendium's own page, starting at
  the moment that matters, with Play / Pause and the answer's other videos. B returns to the game. If a video won't
  play there, Open in Steam's browser is one press away.
- **Pictures and videos without a game running.** Ask about a game from the Steam home screen and the answer still
  gets its video or picture: for the game you name, the conversation's game, or the last one you played.
- **Smoother typing.** Typing a follow-up on the touch keyboard no longer stutters once there's a conversation.
- **Answers come into view.** After you ask, the "thinking" note and then the answer scroll into view by themselves.
- **Lighter on your Deck.** With the Quick Access menu closed, Quest Compendium stops checking the game and the
  question countdown, so Steam stays smooth after a long session. Conversations you switch away from keep only their
  latest screenshot.
- **Performance check.** In Settings: measures how smoothly Steam is drawing for 5 seconds, and the memory Quest
  Compendium holds. Nothing is sent.
- Links open once per press (pressing A on a row could open it twice).

## 0.17.6
- **Check for updates.** In Settings: it tells you you're up to date (with your version), shows the new version with
  Update now, or says it couldn't check.
- **Your 👍 and 👎 now count for pictures and videos.** A 👍 on an answer keeps its picture or video around for
  everyone; a 👎 helps replace one that didn't help.
- **Two styles.** Standard and Roleplay. Min-Max is gone; if you used it, you're on Standard.
- **More accurate Pro answers.** When you ask about something Quest Compendium has already checked, Pro answers use
  those checked facts, so they're faster and get details right more often.

## 0.17.5
- **Worth getting here for any game.** Not just games with a Quest Compendium guide: once Help me here or an answer
  knows where you are, the panel finds what's worth getting there (it says "Finding what's worth getting here…" for
  a moment the first time anyone asks about that area). Only things trusted sources back are listed.
- **Help me here works for every game.** Without a guide it reads the area's name from your screen.
- **Sources under answers.** The wiki and guide pages an answer came from (Fextralife, Fandom, IGN and others), to open
  in Steam's browser.
- **Tidier answers.** The "missable here" box under answers is gone; missables show in Worth getting here.

## 0.17.4
- **Worth getting here.** Under Help me here, for the place you're in: the best gear and spells found there, best first,
  with the ones you can miss badged and when they're gone, plus how many quests start there and who's worth talking to.
  Tick things off as you get them. Baldur's Gate 3 first, more games to follow.
- **Achievements here.** The area's achievements, with the ones a coming point of no return ends listed first.
- Both sections start folded so the panel stays short; open one and it stays open.

## 0.17.3
- **Help me here.** A new button under the game's name: one press looks at your screen, finds where you are in the
  game's guide and what you're doing, and shows a video or picture for it. It says what it found in the conversation,
  or why it couldn't (try again with the map or journal open). It has its own daily allowance, so it doesn't use up
  your questions.
- **Subscribe.** On the free plan, a Subscribe button sits next to Help me here. It shows a QR code: scan it with your
  phone to get Premium, and it shows up on your Deck by itself.

## 0.17.2
- **Wiki pages for the things you ask about.** Ask about a specific item, weapon, spell, character, enemy or place
  ("what does the Blood of Lathander do?") and you get its picture with a link to its wiki page, or the link on its own
  when there's no good picture. The link opens over your game, and B brings you back.

## 0.17.1
- **Videos and pictures show in the side panel.** The video or picture for an answer now appears right under the answer
  in the Quick Access panel, a few seconds after the answer arrives. Before, it only showed when you opened the full
  answer.
- **Voice questions get videos and pictures too.** For a question you asked out loud, the video or picture now matches
  what the answer is about.

## 0.17.0
- **Videos and pictures under answers.** When a video or picture helps, it shows under the answer: a short video for a
  route, a boss fight or a puzzle, a picture for what something looks like or exactly where it is. Nothing is added for
  lore or build questions. **Watch** opens the video full screen over your game, and B brings you back. Other videos
  that cover the same thing are listed below it. **Not right** removes one that doesn't fit.
- **Markers you can trust.** An answer's markers are checked against your screenshot before they settle: anything that
  isn't really there is left out and named instead, and the rest move onto the exact spot.

## 0.16.0
- **Follows the game you start.** Start a different game and Quest Compendium switches to it with "Switched to …" and a
  fresh conversation. Go back to an earlier game and its conversation comes back.
- **More than Steam games.** Non-Steam shortcuts and emulators are recognized too: EmuDeck, ES-DE, RetroArch and
  standalone emulators, including Flatpak and AppImage builds and Windows emulators run through Proton. The game is read
  from the file the emulator loaded, or from RetroArch's history, else the shortcut's own name (without tags like "(GC)").
- **"Which game is this?"** If a file doesn't say which game it is, Quest Compendium asks once and remembers your answer
  for that file.
- **Stays on your Deck.** Detection runs on the Deck. Only the game's name is used.
- **New setting: "Follow the game you start".** Turn it off to keep your current conversation. A **Switch** button
  appears when a different game starts.

## 0.15.4
- **The same guide as the website.** The guide screens now show what questcompendium.com shows: every section, everything
  that's live (no more "coming soon" for places and quests the site already has), the same short names ("Ciri"),
  the kind icons, the walkthrough with its route, choices and advice, and the pictures. When the website's guide gets
  better, the Deck has it the next day.
- **Tidier compendium.** Its sections start folded, each with its count, so the areas stay close at hand.

## 0.15.3
- **Premium yearly.** The Get Premium QR page now gives both plans: $4.99 a month, or $39.99 a year (save 33%). You
  pick on your phone. Already subscribed? Manage your plan there to switch between monthly and yearly.

## 0.15.2
- **Sign in by email.** Under the link QR code, "Or email me a sign-in link": type your address, open the email on
  your phone or PC, confirm the code shown on the Deck, and the Deck signs in by itself. No Google account needed.
- **Premium from the Deck.** Settings shows your plan. "Get Premium" shows a QR code that opens Premium on your phone,
  where you sign in with the same account; Premium then shows up on the Deck by itself. It's also on the
  out-of-questions screen, in place of "About Premium".

## 0.15.1
- **Your questions and the countdown.** The status line shows "Pro 1 · Fast 3 · ⏱ 5:26:41", ticking every second, like
  the desktop app.
- **Quick questions that fit where you are.** Before you ask anything: What should I do next?, Anything I can miss
  here?, and Best gear for where I am (How do I beat this? in a fight). After an answer, two follow-ups. The rest are
  in the pull-down.
- **Hint mode.** "Just a hint" is no longer a default question. Turn on Hint mode in Settings to get it back.

## 0.15.0
- **Missables where you are.** When an answer is about a place our guide covers, it ends with what you could miss
  there for good and why, up to three, with "more in the guide" for the rest. Press one to open the guide at it. Tick
  it off in the guide and it leaves the list. This doesn't use a question.
- **Guide items spotted on screen.** With a screenshot, an answer can now mark things our guide lists in your area
  (in amber, "From the guide"). Ticking one ticks it in the guide too. Answers also know where you last were, so
  they use that place's guide.
- **Update now.** When a new version is out, Update now hands it to Decky, which asks you to confirm and installs
  it. If that doesn't work on your Deck, the reinstall steps show instead.

## 0.14.0
- **Know when there's an update.** When a newer version is out, the panel says so, with How to update: the same
  "Install Plugin from URL" link as before. Your settings and sign-in are kept. Not now hides it until the next one.
- **A cleaner quest log.** Objectives are a few words with one line saying where or how, like the desktop app.
- **Characters, places and collectibles.** Guides list them on the game's page, each with its own page: what it is,
  where to find it (for characters, chapter by chapter), how to get there, shops, services, missions and more. Area
  pages link the ones they mention, and ones still being written are greyed out as coming soon.
- **Search the whole guide.** One search box finds steps, items, characters and places and jumps straight to the
  entry. Apostrophes are optional.
- **When your questions come back.** The status line shows your next top-up, for example "+8 Pro · +25 Fast in
  13h 20m", also under the limit message.
- **Link your account in one step.** Guests see a QR code to link with their phone on their last question of the
  day. Linking keeps your conversation, quest log and guide ticks.
- **Hidden achievements shown plainly**, with their tips, no tapping to reveal.

## 0.13.0
- **Pro or Fast, your pick.** A switch above Send picks the model for your next question, as in the desktop app: Pro
  for the best answers, Fast for quicker ones, each with the questions you have left today. Your pick is remembered.
  When it runs out, the panel switches to the other one with a short note, and back again once it has questions.
- **Questions left, from the server.** The status line shows both counts ("Pro 7 · Fast 24") with the bar for the
  model you're using. The day resets at midnight where you are: the Deck sends its time zone.

## 0.12.0
- **Guides open fast.** The guide list now comes from a small index published with the website (name, cover, page
  count, whether it's checked against sources), so it no longer waits on the server and "The server took too long to
  respond" is gone from the first load. The list is kept on the Deck and shows straight away, refreshing in the
  background. If it can't load, Retry tries again instead of leaving a dead end; guide pages get longer timeouts with
  one automatic retry, and a guide's pages load only when you open it.
- **Your guides first.** The running game's guide is at the top, then the guides you opened recently, then the rest
  A–Z or by popularity (a switch under the search box). Search filters the list as you type. Guides that are fully
  checked against sources say so.
- **Cleaner guide header.** Back with the game's name has its own row, cut to one line with "…" when the name is long,
  and Full screen is a compact button at the end of that row. Left and right move between them on the D-pad.
- **👍 / 👎 under every answer.** Next to Report, in the panel and on the full answer page; a 👎 can say what was wrong
  (wrong place, wrong info, marker off, not helpful), which helps us see which games and questions need work.
- **Hold to talk.** A Hold to talk button in the panel: hold A on it to ask out loud, let go to send it with a
  screenshot (a touch tap starts and stops). It records from the Deck's microphone and only shows when a recorder is
  available.

## 0.11.0
Catches up with the desktop app:
- **New quick questions.** The buttons are now "What should I do next?", "I'm stuck. How do I get past this?",
  "Anything missable here?" and "Help me with this fight", and the More questions pull-down has "What's the best
  choice here?", "Will I miss anything if I leave?", "Is this worth keeping or using?" and "Just a hint, please, no
  spoilers". Each asks for exactly that kind of answer, the same as on the desktop, and the hint questions give a
  gentle nudge without spoiling the solution.
- **New follow-ups.** Under an answer: "What's after that?", "Show me where on screen" (when the answer was about a
  screenshot and one can be taken now; it marks the spots on a fresh one) and "Just a hint instead". "Explain that
  more simply" is gone. All in the desktop app's wording, in every language.
- **Report this answer.** A flag button under every answer opens a short form: offensive or harmful, wrong or
  misleading, or something else, with an optional comment. An answer reported as offensive or harmful is hidden right
  away, with "Show anyway".
- **Missables you can find.** Missable items in guides say where they are starting from something you can find (a
  waypoint, a named character, a landmark or map coordinates) down to the exact container, alongside How and Missable
  because. The area summary box (0.10.1), with directions from a nearby place, fills in as each guide is updated.

## 0.10.1
- **Area summaries.** Guide pages open with a summary box: the region, level range, quests, services and people,
  enemy types, how to get there from a nearby place, which areas it connects to, and map coordinates when the game
  shows them. It appears as each guide is updated.

## 0.10.0
Catches up with the desktop app:
- **Quest log under every answer.** In-game answers now end with a short quest name and 1 to 4 steps: things to do,
  choices (labelled), and warnings in amber, each with the sentence from the answer it comes from. Press A to tick a
  step off; ticks stay with the conversation. The full answer page shows each answer's quest log too.
- **Battle plan.** When the screenshot shows a fight (turn order, End Turn, initiative, enemy health bars), the steps
  become a Battle plan: this turn's action, the kill order and the key tactic.
- **Combat markers.** In a fight, markers cover every important enemy (up to 8) plus the spots the plan uses. The top
  2 or 3 targets are numbered in kill order: red badges, a stronger frame on the screenshot, and "Target 1" in the list.
- **Next turn.** Under a Battle plan, Next turn takes a fresh screenshot and asks what whoever is acting now should
  do, even with screenshots turned off. Its answer replaces the plan.
- **Story beats.** The status strip shows where you are in the story next to the place, as a short phrase
  ("Exploring the crash site of the Nautiloid").
- **How and Missable because.** Guide items show the exact final step to get them (How) and, for missables, what
  locks them out (Missable because).

## 0.9.1
- **Key fights.** Guide pages now have a Key fights section for an area's bosses and big set-piece battles: the
  enemies, their dangerous abilities, weaknesses and resistances, the tactics and positions that win, and the rewards.
  It opens by default, above the enemies list. Guides fill it in as they're updated.

## 0.9.0
Matches the desktop app's answer-first layout:
- **You can see your question being answered.** After you ask, the answer area moves to the top of the panel with
  "You asked: …" and three pulsing dots, and the controller focus goes there too, so the answer is under your thumb
  when it lands. The text box clears right away and the button says "Sending…". If something goes wrong, the error
  shows in the same spot with a **Retry** button.
- **Status strip.** One compact line under the header: the game, where you are, and how many marked spots you've
  collected, with your questions left on the right.
- **Marked spots as a checklist.** Under the answer, each marked spot gets a square numbered badge, its name, and
  where it is. Press A to tick it off; ticks stay with the conversation. The screenshot uses the same square badges.
- **Quest title and missables.** Answers with marked spots show a short quest name, plus an amber "Missable: …" line
  for anything you could still lose for good.
- **Tidier buttons.** Follow-up questions are a row of chips, and Conversation, New and Settings share one row at the
  bottom.
